Overlays and nested scrolling

Apps put scrollable things on top of the viewport: HTML panels, and lists drawn in pixi itself. Scrolling one of those shouldn't zoom the map underneath.

A list drawn in pixi

A pixi object inside the viewport receives the wheel event first. Calling stopPropagation() on it keeps the viewport from zooming (new in v7).

An HTML panel

An HTML element that sits next to the canvas, like the panel above, never sends its wheel events to the viewport. That's the simplest setup: put overlays beside the canvas, not inside the element pixi listens on.

If your overlays have to live inside the element pixi listens on (for example, you called app.renderer.events.setTargetElement(wrapper) on a wrapper around the canvas and your panels), the viewport sees their wheel events too.
